## 1. Universal Hardware Meets Female Biology

Fitness trackers are designed to be universal health platforms for millions of people. They deliver a brilliant, objective foundation of raw data.

The uniqueness of the female body, however, is that our "baseline" isn't the same every day. While the male body operates on a 24-hour rhythm, women go through a dynamic **cycle that individually varies between 21 and 35 days**. Our body temperature, resting heart rate, and recovery ability change naturally throughout the four cycle phases:

1. **Menstrual phase:** The body needs energy for itself, vital signs can fluctuate.
2. **Follicular phase:** Energy rises, the body is extremely resilient.
3. **Ovulation phase:** The absolute performance peak.
4. **Luteal phase:** Body temperature naturally rises due to the hormone progesterone, resting heart rate is often slightly elevated.

When your wearable shows you a slightly elevated resting heart rate during the luteal phase, it's not a sign of poor recovery or oncoming illness – it's simply your biology doing exactly what it should.
